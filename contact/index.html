Contact Oakley 24/7 Plumbing

The fastest way to reach a plumber in Oakley, CA is the phone line, which is answered 24/7 for emergencies and for booking planned work.

Call (855) 575-2890

There is no online form on this site. You can call at any hour or send an email, and this page explains which to choose and what to have ready.

Call for anything urgent

Phone (855) 575-2890 for water spreading across a floor, sewage backing up, a leaking water heater or a drain that has stopped completely. The line runs overnight, on weekends and on holidays, and the dispatcher can talk you through closing valves while a visit is arranged.

Calling also works best for planned jobs, since a conversation about a water heater installation or a slow drain is quicker than a round of messages.

Email for non-urgent questions

For non-urgent questions, write to contact@247plumberoakleyca.com. Email is reasonable for asking about a future project, a question about this site or a request about your information, but it is not monitored the way the phone line is, so do not use it for a flooding emergency.

Repairs happen at your property, so call or email instead of arriving in person.

Talk to dispatch

Water, sewage or a dead water heater is reason enough to call now, whatever the hour on the clock.

Call (855) 575-2890

What to have ready when you call

A few details let the dispatcher size up the job in a minute or two. Have them in front of you or jotted on your phone before you dial, especially if you are standing in water or in the dark.

If you can safely take a photo of the leak, the water heater label or the failed fixture, keep it handy, since it helps both with the visit and with an insurance claim.

  • The street address and the nearest cross street
  • Which fixture or room is affected
  • What you can see: water, sewage, a smell or no hot water
  • What you have already shut off, such as the main valve or a breaker
  • Gate codes, parking notes or a pet that will be in the house
  • A phone number where you can be reached during the visit

Safety comes before the call

A gas odor is not a plumbing call. Exit the building, dial 911, and contact PG&E at 1-800-743-5000 first. Do not flip switches or use a lighter on the way out.

For a flood, close the main water valve first and kill power to wet areas. Our service area page lists the towns we cover, and the Brentwood page covers that city's calls.

What happens after you call

The dispatcher confirms your address, repeats back the problem and says whether a visit tonight or a same-day appointment when available fits better. On site, the plumber explains what failed and gives a clear price before work starts.

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes, and our privacy policy explains how we handle the details you share.

Dispatch is open

Have your address and a short description ready, and the dispatcher will take it from there.
