Private lateral, public main

A sewer system has two parts. The lateral is the pipe from your house to the sewer in the street, and it belongs to the property owner. The main runs along the street and belongs to Ironhouse Sanitary District.

The difference decides who clears a blockage. A backup inside one house usually points to the lateral or a branch drain. Backups in several nearby houses at once suggest the main, and that is a call to the district at (925) 625-2279.

What happens on a sewer call

We start by asking where water backs up first, since a floor drain or a bathtub is often the lowest point in the house. Then a cable or hydro jetting clears the line, and a camera confirms what was causing the blockage.

If the camera shows damage, the footage is what you decide on. Keep that footage for a home sale. Ironhouse Sanitary District asks for a CCTV Compliance Certificate when a property more than 20 years old is sold.

Before you pour anything down the drain

Skip acid and caustic drain cleaners. They are hazardous to handle and to the plumber who arrives afterward, and they rarely clear a root or grease blockage.

While you wait for the visit

Small steps limit the mess.

Stop the water

Avoid flushing and running dishwashers or washers when drains are backing up.

Protect the lowest floor

Move rugs and belongings away from floor drains, tubs and showers.
