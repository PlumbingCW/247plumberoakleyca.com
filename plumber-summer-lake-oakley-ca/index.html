Hard water and what it leaves behind

Diablo Water District reports hardness between 87 and 150 mg/L, with an average near 124 mg/L. That is about 7 grains per gallon, which is moderately hard. Over the years it leaves white scale on faucet aerators, showerheads and the bottom of tank water heaters.

Scale is a normal result of the water supply and not a defect in your house. A quick flush of the tank, clean aerators and a look at the anode rod slow it down. If the scale bothers you, water softening and filtration is the planned fix.

Water heater rules worth knowing

California requires water heaters to be braced or strapped so they do not tip in an earthquake, under Health and Safety Code section 19211. A replacement should meet that requirement, and you can check your current unit by looking for two straps around the upper and lower thirds of the tank.

For gas units, the Bay Area Air Quality Management District adopted amendments on November 4, 2026 that delay its zero-NOx rule for new residential gas units to January 2028. Read our water heater replacement page for how that affects the choice between gas and electric.

  • Know where the main water shutoff and the heater's own shutoff valve are before you need them
  • Do not run a heater that is leaking at the tank, shut off the water and the power or gas
  • Take a photo of the data plate, which shows the age and capacity, before you call
