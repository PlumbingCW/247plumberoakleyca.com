Magnolia Park is a residential area in central Oakley, ZIP 94561. City environmental records list it as a residential and mixed-use development. We have no reliable record of when each street was built, so this page does not guess at pipe materials or ages.

The most useful thing for a Magnolia Park homeowner is knowing who is responsible for what. A leak before the meter, a leak after it and a sewer backup are three different calls, and this page sorts them out.

Start with your water bill

Diablo Water District supplies most of Oakley, and the City of Brentwood supplies some areas near the Brentwood boundary. Your water bill names your supplier, and that is the quickest way to know whose line runs to your meter.

Do not rely on a map or on a neighbor's answer. Two houses on the same street can occasionally be served differently near a boundary, so the bill is the evidence.

Who handles which water problem

The usual split in California is simple. The supplier owns the line to the meter and the homeowner owns everything after it.

Know these three spots before an emergency

Find them on a calm day and show everyone in the house.

The main shutoff

Usually near where the line enters the house or at the meter box. Turn it fully to close the water to the whole property.

Fixture shutoffs

Toilets and sinks have small wall valves. Closing one stops a single fixture without losing water everywhere.

The water heater valve

The cold inlet valve on top of the tank stops water into the heater when it leaks. See water heater repair for what comes next.

Sewer side, in one paragraph

Ironhouse Sanitary District runs the public sewer, and the homeowner owns the private lateral from the house to the main. If the district sees a backup, it can say whether the main is clear. Our drain cleaning page covers clearing the private side, and a camera shows what is in the line.
