Downtown Oakley is the commercial and civic center of the city, ZIP 94561. The City of Oakley's offices are at 3231 Main Street, and the Main Street corridor was rebuilt with new sidewalks, drainage, sanitary sewer work and lighting. That work won a 2018 regional public works award.

A mixed district calls for a different angle than a housing tract. Businesses need minimal downtime, and the homes nearby need the usual repairs. This page covers both.

A mixed district: homes, shops and offices

Downtown buildings differ widely in age and use. We do not assume any particular pipe material from the address. A technician identifies it on site, and a camera run through a drain shows its real condition.

A restaurant, a salon and a house all use water differently. Grease, hair and wipes cause very different blockages, so the first question is always what the building is used for.

Commercial tenants: know who owns the pipe

In a shared building, the landlord often controls the main drain and the water supply, and the tenant controls the fixtures inside the unit. Check your lease before authorizing work, and tell the landlord when a main line is involved.

When a drain is slow throughout a building, a camera inspection finds out whether the blockage is in your branch or the building's main line, and see sewer inspection for how that works.
