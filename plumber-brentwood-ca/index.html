Before We Arrive: Steps for a Brentwood Home

Close the main water valve

Look for the main shutoff where the water line enters the house, often on an exterior wall, a garage wall or near the heater. Turn it clockwise until it stops, then open a low faucet to bleed off pressure.

Kill power to wet areas

If water has reached a garage, laundry room or kitchen outlet, flip that breaker at the panel and stay out of the standing water until the circuit is off.

Clear the work area

Move cars from the garage bay, lift rugs and clear the path to the water heater, the sink cabinet or the cleanout so access takes minutes and not half an hour.
